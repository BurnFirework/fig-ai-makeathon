# Product inspiration

These references inform Unfold's image-first canvas, spatial grouping, and card controls.

- **PureRef** keeps the board visible and puts labels and actions close to the selected image. Unfold follows that approach with large images, a visible Add action, and card actions available on selection or keyboard focus. [PureRef 2.0 examples](https://www.pureref.com/blog/pureref2/) · [Image focus](https://www.pureref.com/handbook/images/#focus)
- **Apple Freeform** arranges images, text, and reference material together on an open canvas. Unfold keeps notes and sourced steps near their inspiration and uses links to show their relationships. [Freeform board examples](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/12/apple-launches-freeform-a-powerful-new-app-designed-for-creative-collaboration/)
- **Allume**, formerly Muse, uses a small set of visible tools and nested boards for organizing material. Its layout is a reference for giving content most of the canvas. [Allume](https://allume.com/) · [Muse board example](https://media.museapp.com/website/2024/inbox-hero.webp)

The November 7, 2026 review used official documentation and published examples. It did not include hands-on comparison or usability testing.
