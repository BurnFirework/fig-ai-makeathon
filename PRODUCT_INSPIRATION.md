# Product inspiration

Research date: November 7, 2026. Selected direction: PureRef, Apple Freeform, and Allume (formerly Muse). These are the sole product references for Unfold's design direction, as chosen by the user after the initial research.

## Design direction

- **PureRef:** image-first canvas, minimal surrounding UI, and details on demand.
- **Apple Freeform:** natural composition of images, text, and other media with lightweight spatial grouping.
- **Allume:** restrained tools, capture inbox, and nested boards that let ideas grow.

Together, these establish a content-led spatial workspace: large images, readable ideas, quiet controls, and organization that grows with the board. These are design judgments, not measured usability results.

## Selected references

### 1. PureRef: let the board occupy the application

Inspect the [product demo](https://www.pureref.com/) and [2.0 UI examples](https://www.pureref.com/blog/pureref2/).

The published board composition presents images and small group labels on a neutral surface. Its [settings](https://www.pureref.com/handbook/settings/) document a title bar that defaults to appearing on mouseover, with options for always, movement-only, or never. The 2.0 release includes a command palette and comments for information that need not remain visible. Its [image handbook](https://www.pureref.com/handbook/images/#focus) documents Space or double-click to focus selected images and return to the previous view.

**Borrow:** direct image placement; optional labels; details on demand; quick focus and return. For Unfold, study how much surrounding card furniture can disappear while preserving the image and its meaning.

**Tradeoff:** hidden controls have a discovery cost. Keep a clear Add entry point and show editing actions on selection or keyboard focus, with hover as an additional shortcut.

### 2. Apple Freeform: mixed-media composition without a dashboard

Inspect [official board imagery](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/12/apple-launches-freeform-a-powerful-new-app-designed-for-creative-collaboration/) and the [current Mac guide](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/freeform/welcome/mac).

Apple documents mixed-media placement, drag-and-drop, alignment guides, and inline previews. The published examples show photographs, documents, labels, and drawings sharing an open canvas.

**Borrow:** spatial grouping, ordinary text headings, and reference previews that remain in context. Unfold's answers can sit beside their inspiration without every item becoming a complex application panel.

**Tradeoff:** its extensive drawing and shape capabilities serve a broader whiteboard. A vision board needs a smaller visible tool set.

### 3. Allume, formerly Muse: let ideas grow inside boards

Inspect the [current product and screenshots](https://allume.com/) and this [older Muse inspiration-board image](https://media.museapp.com/website/2024/inbox-hero.webp).

The current official site confirms the name change, nested boards, an inbox at the left edge, and support for images, links, notes, PDFs, video, and ink. It deliberately omits complex text formatting and drawing brushes.

**Borrow:** a temporary place for newly captured material; nested themes; a restrained tool set. Nested boards are a useful precedent for keeping Unfold's growing branches comprehensible.

**Tradeoff:** nesting creates navigation work. Keep the first board immediate; introduce depth when there is meaningful content to organize. Treat older Muse screenshots as historical visual references.

## Application to Unfold

The following are proposed directions, not a completed UI audit or implemented changes:

1. **Images:** display imagery with minimal wrapping. Keep a caption when it adds the user's intent; move filenames and technical metadata into details.
2. **Text:** retain readable questions and answers. Remove repeated labels that only announce the content type when the layout already makes it clear.
3. **Actions:** maintain a small stable Add/navigation area; expose node-specific actions on selection and keyboard focus. Avoid requiring hover alone.
4. **Connections:** preserve their meaning while making inactive lines and ports visually quiet. Emphasize the selected branch and connection-creation state.
5. **Agent activity:** prioritize the useful question, suggestion, or action in the board; provide detailed process evidence through an accessible activity view.
6. **Visual hierarchy:** let photos provide color. Reserve accent color, borders, shadows, and motion for selection, interaction, and meaningful feedback.

## Evidence and limitations

Research used official product sites, documentation, and published product imagery. PureRef's published board example was visually inspected. Freeform and Allume findings rely on official documentation and published media; no authenticated product sessions or comparative usability tests were performed. Older Muse imagery is labeled as historical.
